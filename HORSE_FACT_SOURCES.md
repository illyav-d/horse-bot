# Sources for the new horse facts

These references support the 30 facts added in v1.8.4. Facts are paraphrased for the bot; source links stay here so chat messages remain short.

- **Whiskers and sensory hairs:** [British Horse Society — Sensory hairs](https://www.bhs.org.uk/horse-care-and-welfare/health-care-management/horse-health/sensory-hairs/). Muzzle awareness, sensitive follicles, food texture, and the protective blink response.
- **Walking, trotting, cantering, and galloping:** [WSU Extension — Beginning Horsemanship](https://pubs.extension.wsu.edu/product/beginning-horsemanship-mm/), [UGA Cooperative Extension — Evaluating Common Equine Performance Classes](https://extension.uga.edu/publications/detail.html?number=B1401&title=evaluating-common-equine-performance-classes), and [Iowa State University — Horseless Horse](https://www.extension.iastate.edu/hamilton/files/documents/ISU_4H_HorselessHorse2003_EmailQ.pdf).
- **Coat colours and markings:** [University of Kentucky — Equine Coat Color Genetics 101](https://equine.mgcafe.uky.edu/news-story/equine-coat-color-genetics-101), [Virginia Tech — A Horse of a Different Color](https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/content/pubs_ext_vt_edu/en/380/380-104/380-104.html), and [USDA APHIS — Equine Identification](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/nvap/reference-guide/animal-identification/equine).
- **Lipizzan foal colours:** [Lipica Stud Farm — Coexistence brochure](https://www.lipica.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/brosura-ang-tisk.pdf).
- **Shetland pony sizes and uses:** [Shetland Pony Stud-Book Society — The Pony Today](https://www.shetlandponystudbooksociety.co.uk/about-the-breed/pony-today/). Height categories refer to this UK registry, not every international Shetland registry.
- **Winter coats, daylight, and heat from forage fermentation:** [University of Minnesota Extension — Caring for your horse in the winter](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/animals-and-livestock/horse/caring-for-your-horse-in-the-winter).
- **Mutual grooming and social opportunities:** [World Horse Welfare — Stereotypic horse behaviours](https://eu.worldhorsewelfare.org/advice/stereotypic-horse-behaviours).
- **Recognising people through faces and voices:** [Nakamura et al. — Horses cross-modally recognize women and men](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9995451/), including its discussion of earlier familiar-person experiments.
- **Human voices and body postures:** [University of Sussex — Emotional awareness in horses](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/research/labs/mammal-communication-and-cognition/research/horses-emotional-awareness).
- **Friends, forage, and freedom:** [World Horse Welfare — Horse management essentials](https://eu.worldhorsewelfare.org/index.php/advice/horse-management-essentials).
